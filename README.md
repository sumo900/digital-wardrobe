# Digital Wardrobe

**[digital-wardrobe](https://sumo900.github.io/digital-wardrobe/)**

A interactive outfit visualization tool that helps you explore clothing combinations. Mix and match shirts and pants beforehand, no more last-minute chaos.

## How to Use

**Desktop:**
- Press **A** or **D** to cycle through shirts
- Press **W** or **S** to cycle through pants

**Mobile:**
- Swipe **left/right** on a shirt or pant to switch items
